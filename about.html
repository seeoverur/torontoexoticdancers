Who We Are

Private entertainment without making the booking complicated.

Planning a private party already comes with enough moving pieces. Finding entertainment should not become another full-time job.

That's why 6ix Gentlemen focuses on making the process straightforward. Tell us your date, location, occasion and the type of atmosphere you're planning. Our booking team helps match the event with an entertainer and show format appropriate for your group.

Whether you're organizing an intimate bachelorette party in downtown Toronto, a birthday celebration in Mississauga, or a larger private event elsewhere in the GTA, our goal is the same: clear communication, professional performers and an experience designed around the guest of honor.
